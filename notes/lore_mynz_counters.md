Mynzian Creature Counters (Canon)
=================================

Doctrine
--------
- Counters are negotiations with inevitability. If a counter works, it cannot be reused that day. Mynz remembers.

Predators (recap)
-----------------
- Small theropod analogues; learn within minutes; hunt by intercept geometry (vibration, pressure, thermal contrast, pattern repetition).

Counters
--------
- The Cutters (paired flankers; kill on hesitation)
  - Counter: Asymmetric Delay
  - Tools: Drop-Wedges (2), Groundskin Pads
  - Method: drop one wedge behind, place one ahead, rotate pads mid-step.
  - Effect: trajectory misread; commit angle fails; predators disengage.
  - Cost: lose wedges; must vacate area. Failure: repeating pattern = death.

- The Threaders (burrow/ankle interceptors; pressure-driven)
  - Counter: False Weight
  - Tools: Ghostline Cord, Shard Compass
  - Method: stretch ghostline under light tension; compass confirms misalignment.
  - Effect: strike hits empty ground; predator exposes then retreats.
  - Cost: ghostline severed; area unsafe after minutes. Failure: standing still = death.

- The Listeners (sound-memory predators; rare)
  - Counter: Silence with Misdirection
  - Tools: Blindfire Pebble, Ashveil fibers
  - Method: drop blindfire at feet; move before bloom; shed fibers to false trail.
  - Effect: listener tracks cloud, loses you.
  - Cost: cloak lost; no second chance. Failure: throwing pebble (implies targeting).

- The Watchers (high-ground sentinels; signalers)
  - Counter: Non-Resolution
  - Tools: none
  - Method: stop, match terrain, wait until interest fades.
  - Effect: disengage; area neutral.
  - Cost: time, exposure to others. Failure: fleeing while observed.

Encounter Example (Field Entry Fragment)
----------------------------------------
- Snapblade breaks under pressure; drop-wedge slips; pads adjusted too late; ankle brush (test); shard compass spins from vibration; blindfire blooms fast; wind shifts; ashveil tears; stopping instinctively; sound moves away; ghostline disappears (taken); no bodies/blood—just fewer tools; immediate retreat.
- City annotation: “equipment malfunction due to environmental stress.”
- Wild addendum: “The island let him go because he learned fast enough.”

System Notes
------------
- Gear failure is part of survival; counters buy exit, never victory.
- Ties to Thal path → Wild sub-path availability.
- Journal tone: shorter entries, tool-loss noted, no heroics.
