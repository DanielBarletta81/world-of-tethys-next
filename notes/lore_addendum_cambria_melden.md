Canonical Lore Addendum — Cambria, Melden, & Deep Time
======================================================

1. Cambria’s Temporal Position (Locked)
---------------------------------------
- Cambria predates Melden by generations; it is Old Tethysia, a previous “answer” to the questions Sky City later claims to solve.
- Cambria fell during a major tidal surge compounded by seismic instability, sudden creature displacement, and rising waters that erased infrastructure faster than evacuation.
- Cambria is Atlantis-like without myth: towers toppled, murals shattered, flora/fauna statues submerged; civic memory drowned, not destroyed. Stone, biology, and pattern survived.

2. Melden’s Youth & Elder Matsu (Correction)
--------------------------------------------
- Young Melden worked with Elder Matsu during early New Tethysia, pre-Triumvirate, before class stratification, before “Sky City” had political weight.
- Matsu embodied generational Cambria memory, humility before older systems, and the stance that cities must listen before they organize.
- Melden never mythologized elevation; he saw cities as temporary scaffolds, not achievements.

3. Old Tethysia / Cambria (Expanded)
------------------------------------
- Collapse accelerated by creature panic, reef migration, and displacement.
- Art and identity centered flora/fauna reverence; now preserved as underwater oyster statues, calcified reliefs, reef-grown effigies.
- Symbolic resonance with deep time (≈100–111 Ma) as metaphor, not literal dating. Cambria is the nearest, most accessible lost layer; Sky City refuses full acknowledgment.

4. The Cambrian Layer (Critical Concept)
----------------------------------------
- A geological/biological/cultural palimpsest hiding multiple lost civilizations; mineralization + organism collaboration preserve structures.
- Catastrophic reshaping selectively resurfaces pieces. Cambria is first revealed, not last.

5. Post–Book 2 Reveal Justification
-----------------------------------
- Book 2 Watcher event reveals, not expands: coastlines redraw, sediment shifts, submerged structures re-emerge. Map “zooms out” because access increases.
- New regions, shallows, ruins, biomes surface without moving canonical centers.

6. Melden’s Legacy Reframed
---------------------------
- He remembered Cambria without nostalgia; treated New Tethysia as provisional; knew cities fail upward; saw Sky City’s sin as believing it was final.
- He couldn’t be voted out; had to be removed and softened because he carried pre-City memory into City power.

7. Arc Payoff
-------------
- Book 1: denial/containment.
- Book 2: exposure/reshaping.
- Book 3: reckoning with deep time.
- Sky City’s tragedy is amnesia, not corruption alone.
