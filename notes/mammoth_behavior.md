# WHAT THE MAMMOTH DOES — WATCHER FLATS (PRE-ERUPTION)

Behavior-only record; no dramatics. Mammoth acts on inevitability awareness (pressure gradients, long-period tremors, subsonic vibration, migratory memory).

The mammoth does not trumpet. It stands with one forefoot lifted, resting on nothing, as if waiting for the ground to finish a sentence. Steam drifts across its flank. It does not flinch.

A small ash-colored fox-thing crosses the flats. The mammoth watches it pass, then turns its head slightly. The fox veers. Not in panic. Just enough.

The mammoth lowers its foot. The ground settles.

It shifts weight subtly, aligning its mass along a line no one else sees. Tail still; ears not raised. Not alarm—calculation.

A distant rumble passes beneath the flats: too low to hear, too broad to feel unless you are large enough to belong to it. The mammoth exhales, long and even. Birds circling drift higher without deciding to.

The mammoth takes one step—not forward, but sideways. Stone cracks where the step might have been. It does not look back.

Later, when ash begins to fall in earnest, the mammoth does not flee. It turns slowly toward an older path it has not walked in years. Grass does not grow there anymore. That does not matter.

It moves only when the ground finishes speaking. By the time humans notice the tremor, the mammoth is already gone. Not running. Not escaping. Remembering.

## World Logic
- Mammoths are not prophetic; they do not predict eruptions—they respond to inevitability.
- They are tuned to pressure gradients, long-period tremors, subsonic vibration, ancient migratory memory.
- They do not warn others because warning requires interpretation, and interpretation introduces error. They simply move correctly.

## Beliefs
- Wild/Thal (unwritten): “The ground remembers through them.”
- City (unnoted): records “Delayed migration” and “Atypical behavior,” missing direction, timing, and absence of panic.

## Core Tethys Truth
> The loudest stories are told by those who survive them. The most important ones are told by those who move in time.
