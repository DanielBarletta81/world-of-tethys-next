# VR Rumor Space (Murmur Field) v1

Purpose: Physicalize rumor cognition in VR without exposing graph mechanics.

## Core Concept

- Rumors are felt as currents, echoes, partial structures, unstable landmarks.
- Player never sees nodes, edges, NPC IDs, or faction names.
- Experience is density, resistance, pull, and interference.

## Data Mapping (Hard Spec)

RumorFlow
- originNpcId
- strength (confidence)
- distortion
- faction
- age

NPC Resonance Anchor
- Stable NPC: clear harmonic tone
- Unstable NPC: phase-shifted echo
- Collapsing NPC: interference/dropouts

Player Interference Body
- Movement bends rumor flows
- Stillness clarifies structure, not content
- Motion increases noise

## Interaction Rules

- No grabbing, rewinding, or isolating a single voice
- Stillness reveals structure, not truth
- Observation induces measurement collapse

## Betrayal Manifestation

- Collapse destabilizes anchor resonance
- Tone becomes dissonant
- Orientation loss precedes real-world betrayal

## Export Schema (Renderer Target)

VRRumorNode
- npcId
- position
- resonanceStability
- factionBias
- activeRumors: RumorFlow[]
