# CITY PROPAGANDA POSTERS + FORGED HYBRID SEAL

Production-ready systems, not one-offs. City voice: authoritative, symmetric, reassuring, coercive.

## Poster Type I — Stability Notice
- Purpose: reassure mid-tiers post-Melden; normalize emergency power.
- Hierarchy: City seal (dominant, centered) → “STABILITY IS MAINTAINED” → “UNDER EMERGENCY CORD” → “RECORDED BY THE CHAMBER.”
- Grid: centered stack with generous margins; perfect centering; no dates; no handwriting.
- Wrong tells: heavy symmetry, wasteful whitespace, timeless authority tone.

## Poster Type II — Oath Reminder
- Purpose: bind new citizens/graduates emotionally and legally.
- Layout: seal top-crown; “REMEMBER YOUR CORD” → “THE CITY PROVIDES / THE CITY RECORDS” → “FAILURE IS REMEMBERED” → “— OFFICE OF RECORD.”
- Manipulation: memory as threat; no punishment stated; seal implies inevitability.

## Poster Type III — Investigation Announcement
- Purpose: perform justice without doing it.
- Layout: seal as watermark; “AN INVESTIGATION / IS UNDERWAY” → “REMAIN CALM” → “THE RECORD IS IN ORDER.”
- Trick: passive voice, no subject, no timeline; seal substitutes for evidence.

## Material / Color
- Paper: ash-gray, fibrous. Ink: near-black, slightly warm. Seal: embossed/over-inked. Wear: none (City propaganda never looks damaged).

## Forged Hybrid Seal (City imitation of the true mark)
- Intent: City saw the true correspondent seal and “fixes” it—closing breaks, adding authority ring, regularizing curves.
- SVG (drop-in): see `public/icons/hybrid_seal.svg`.
- Appears on: revised Melden records; “recovered” field notes; sanitized expedition summaries; City maps of the wild.
- Never on: original journals; survivor correspondence; anything Ravel/Wild touches.
- Read: almost right, but controlled; negative space is contained; authority asserted administratively.

## Fracture Note
The more ornate the seal, the less reliable the record. The City copies symbols it does not understand; that is how you recognize the forgery.
