# Tethys Atlas (252 to 111 Ma) - Factions, Biomes, Microbiomes, Navigation

Scope: Consolidated worldbuilding atlas derived from the three local PDFs. This is a structured narrative layer with explicit ties to the research points in those documents.

Sources (local PDFs)
- Tethyan Worldbuilding_ Biomes and Evolution.pdf
- Tethys Ocean Research and Navigation Simulation.pdf
- Paleogeography, Microbiology, and Gigantism.pdf

Timeframe and Climate Baselines
- 252 to 111 Ma: transition from Late Permian into Early Cretaceous.
- Super-greenhouse climate, high CO2, high sea level, no polar ice caps.
- Tethys acts as equatorial heat engine with westward circumglobal current and paired gyres.
- Strong stratification: freshwater surface lid over warm, saline, low-oxygen deep water.

Core Biomes and Geological Analogs
1) Ironwood Domain (Araucarian megaforest)
   - Analog formations: Wealden Group floodplains; Patagonian fossil forests.
   - Vegetation: Araucaria, Agathis, Podocarps; Cheirolepidiaceae along the coast.
   - Micro-biomes:
     - Canopy emergent platforms (60m+ canopy).
     - Braided-river log jam rafts.
     - Coastal conifer "mangroves" (Frenelopsis).

2) Gargantua Domain (volcanic island arc)
   - Analog formation: Kohistan-Ladakh intra-oceanic arc.
   - Terrain: stratovolcano chains, ash fields, pumice rafts, obsidian slopes.
   - Micro-biomes:
     - Caldera rims and ash plains.
     - Lava tubes and flank caves.
     - Pumice drift lanes and ashfall shelves.

3) Rookery Domain (carbonate platform and cliffs)
   - Analog formations: Apulia carbonate platform; Hateg Island.
   - Terrain: karst cliffs, caves with upward ventilation, shallow platform lagoons.
   - Micro-biomes:
     - Karst cave networks.
     - Reef gaps and tidal channels.
     - Shallow "wadeable" platform tops.

Microbial and Chemical Ecology
- OAEs create photic-zone euxinia: hydrogen sulfide rises into the lighted zone.
- Green sulfur bacteria (Chlorobiaceae) signal toxic purple or red water.
- Viral shunt locks productivity into microbial loop, limiting higher trophic transfer.
- Sulfate-reducing bacteria dominate deep water, emitting H2S.

Hypercane Atmosphere and Storm Physics (ancient weather layer)
- Hypercane threshold: SST ~50°C; requires localized super-heating beyond background Tethys warmth.
- Trigger mechanisms: bolide impact into shallow shelves; LIP eruptions in restricted platforms.
- Core dynamics: sub-700 hPa central pressure; sustained winds >800 km/h; stratospheric injection of water vapor and sea salts.
- Ozone collapse: chlorine photochemistry leads to extreme UV exposure after storm passage.
- OAE coupling: hypercane mixing brings H2S-rich deep water to surface, causing toxic fog and purple-water blooms.

Megaflora Failure and Rafting (storm ecology)
- Araucaria/Wollemia: longitudinal splintering under hypercane loads; shallow root plates fail in saturated surge soils.
- Frenelopsis mangroves: low drag, but catastrophic fragmentation under >50 m surge; forests become abrasive sediment.
- Equisetum giants: hollow, silica-reinforced; buckle locally but raft well in surge, enabling dispersal.

Earth-Tune and Seismic Hearing (lithospheric acoustics)
- Limestone platforms: low attenuation; long-range “ringing” for seismic and microseism cues.
- Volcanic arcs: vesicular basalts act as low-pass filters, preserving infrasonic precursors.
- Biological route lore: bone-conduction hearing evolves to read infrasound; “Earth Tune” guides safe travel.

Silurian Lineage Additions (aquatic hominid feasibility)
- Rapid fixation of diving traits (PDE10A splenomegaly) is plausible at Tethys timescales.
- Nasal drift: dorsal migration of nares to mid-skull supports surface breathing with minimal exposure.
- Bone-conduction hearing: hypertrophied ossicles or mandibular pads for seismic and infrasonic cues.
- Dense bone (pachyosteosclerosis): ballast for bottom-walking in mangrove swamps.

Deltas, Rivers, and Sedimentary Context
- North African Paleo-Nile and Nubian systems: huge deltaic complexes, quartz-rich sands.
- Khorat Basin: red beds + massive evaporites from seasonal aridity and basin isolation.
- Pontides margin: steep arc shelves, turbidite fans, submarine canyon collapses.
- Wealden floodplains: braided rivers, log jams, lagoonal swamps, wildfire cycles.

Rivers, Tiers, and Mixing Zones (user-defined world layer)
- Danian River: connects Mystics and Ironwoods; flows along Shastea Adjacent and Ordovic.
- Danian splits: branches into Silurian wetland swamps and estuaries.
- Cimmerian branch: splits off and dumps over Sky City in the southwest map quadrant.
- Higher Tiers to Lower Tiers: stepped descent "down to ledge and out" into open sea.
- Open-sea mixing zone: cooler Cimmerian water mixes with warmer surface flow, creating a benthic + pelagic nutrient hotspot that concentrates fish, larger predators, and apex hunters.

River and Faction Linkages (logical extensions)
- Mystics: custodians of Danian headwaters; ritualized control of seasonal flow and flood timing.
- Ironwoods: rely on Danian flood pulses for cone/seed masting cycles and river-raft trade.
- Silurians: dominate the Danian splits in wetland swamps and estuaries; manage tidal gates and brackish nurseries.
- Sky City: Cimmerian branch spillover creates a vertical “mist fall” zone; canopy foraging shifts to fish and scavenged drift.
- Thals: intermittent access to nutrient pulses from the mixing zone via pumice lanes and ashfall shelves.

Seasonal Event Calendar (river and ocean cadence)
- Flood Pulse (Danian Rise): wet-season surge; Ironwood masting and fish runs peak.
- Slack Window Week: megatide slack aligns; long crossings and market convergence.
- Purple Water Warning: photic-zone euxinia blooms; estuary closures and offshore retreat.
- Fog Season: H2S degassing and aerosol haze; stilt cities go vertical, caves preferred.
- Ashfall Weeks: arc eruptions; Thal routes shift to lava tubes and pumice lanes.
- Glow-Tide Bloom: biolum band expands; predator gatherings and filter harvesting.
- Hyper-Storm Window: extreme cyclones scour air; post-storm travel window opens.
- Cimmerian Cool-Down: cold branch strengthens; mixing-zone bounty spikes.
- Ozone Scar: UV-scorched interval after hypercane stratospheric injection; surface travel restricted.
- Toxic Fog Pulse: H2S release after deep mixing; low-lying routes closed.

Navigation Guidelines (111 Ma operational layer)
- Bathymetry dominates: microplate archipelago with rapid platform drowning.
- Westward Circumglobal Tethys Current: 2 to 4 knots, fast westbound travel.
- Iberian bottleneck: water piles up, promotes stratification and anoxia.
- Megatides: 6 to 8 m in funnel straits; tidal currents 2 to 3 m/s.
- Tidal discipline: travel on flood or ebb; wait for short slack windows.
- Haze and cloud cover: swell refraction and bioluminescence cues replace stars.
- Toxic fog: H2S degassing and sulfur aerosols; avoid low-lying shorelines.
- Hyper-storm risk: storm corridors can temporarily clear toxic air.

Faction Placement and Micro-Biomes
Sky City (arboreal canopy faction)
- Biome: Ironwood megaforest.
- Micro-biomes: canopy emergents, log-raft channels, coastal conifer mangroves.
- Constraints: predation on forest floor, wildfire cycles, high canopy exposure.

Thals (volcanic arc faction)
- Biome: Gargantua arc.
- Micro-biomes: ash plains, obsidian slopes, lava tubes, caldera rims.
- Constraints: ashfall toxicity, PDC risk, seismic collapse, resource scarcity.

Silurians (semi-aquatic interface faction)
- Biome: Rookery platform and cliffs.
- Micro-biomes: karst caves, shallow platform tops, tidal channels.
- Constraints: euxinia, toxic fog, megatides, drowning platforms.

Additional Human Possibility Variants (compatible with PDF constraints)
- Platform Walkers: shallow-draft or amphibious travel on platform tops.
- Tidal Bore Riders: time straits for megatide propulsion.
- Purple-Water Exiles: avoid photic-zone euxinia; live on margins.
- Fog-Top Settlers: stilt cities above dense H2S layers.
- Pumice Mariners: navigation through ash and pumice lanes.
- Swell Readers: rely on swell refraction and bioluminescent cues.
- Storm Migrants: seasonal relocations to avoid hyper-storm corridors.

Gigantism and Biomechanics (environmental consequences)
- Sauropods: air-sac pneumaticity, giant heel pads, heat-dumping respiratory system.
- Spinosaurus: osteosclerosis for ballast; paddle tail for aquatic pursuit.
- Marine reptiles: thunniform locomotion for long-range efficiency.
- Heteromorph ammonites: drift-feeding in low-energy stratified waters.

Biological "Technologies" as World Anchors
- Desalination: Frenelopsis uses xylem tension and salt glands to drink brine.
- Optics: calcite (Iceland spar) lenses and polarization detection enable sunstone navigation.

Relict Permian Desert Thread (worldbuilding overlay)
- The PDFs establish a 252 to 111 Ma continuity and repeated arid/hypersaline episodes.
- Use the Khorat red-bed and evaporite basin model as a long-lived desert relic zone.

Tethys Naming Doctrine (survival speech + symbol logic)
- Spoken names are short, functional, and immediate in the wild.
- Moon = Watcher. Sun = Giver. Desert = Taker. Volcano = Watcher Volcano.
- Most route labels are two to three words: noun + function + place.
- Sky City retains formal, ornate versions in writing; field speech stays clipped.
- Carvings, glyphs, and sign sequences carry the full meaning outside shelter.

Core Name Tokens (fast speech)
- Watcher: moon, night guidance, time.
- Giver: sun, warmth, growth, clarity.
- Taker: desert, loss, extraction, attrition.
- Mouth: river entry/exit, estuary.
- Split: branch point.
- Drop: ledge/fall into open water.
- Gate: controlled passage.
- Breath: safe air, vented cave.
